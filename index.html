Before you begin

How this works

This is not an examination and it is not part of your performance rating. Your individual answers will not be shown to your supervisor. An honest low rating is far more useful to us than a polite high one. Takes about 25–30 minutes.

For every ability statement you will give two ratings.

What I can do nowHow much my work needs it
1No knowledge or experience of this at allNot relevant to my role
2Aware of it, but cannot yet do it on my ownSlightly important
3Can do it with guidance or supervisionModerately important
4Can do it independently and consistentlyVery important
5Can do it well and can coach othersCritical — I cannot deliver my targets without it
