Before you begin

What is submitted here, and what is not

Fill in your pre-work pack on paper first, then copy the relevant answers here. When you finish, this page saves a file to your device — send that file to your Provincial Link. You must still bring your paper copy to the workshop; you will work from it across both days.
Tasks 2 and 3 are not submitted here. Your stakeholder list names officials, and your case brief carries household detail. Both stay with you on paper. For Task 3 we ask only for the case code and the type of blockage, so that triads can be arranged.

Data privacy

Your name is optional. Answers are consolidated by your Provincial Link and the RPMO and reported only in aggregate — no individual answer is shown to a supervisor. Responses are held only for this programme cycle and its follow-through, then deleted. You may skip any item, and you may ask for your response to be removed at any time through your Provincial Link.

Administered by Great Life Foundation, Inc. on behalf of DSWD Field Office IX. Questions go to your Provincial Link or the Field Office Data Protection Officer.
